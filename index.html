How it works: A scheduled job collects two public feeds about every 15 minutes and publishes one shared snapshot, so every visitor sees the same trends (you don't need to keep the page open). FirstEnergy (Ohio Storm Center) reports customers out per county and per city/township; Cleveland Public Power reports which distribution feeders are out plus an approximate count of affected accounts (no per-customer counts). "% restored" and the restoration-rate figures are measured against the peak observed since the current storm began; the shared history auto-advances — when a storm is restored it's archived to the Storms log and the next one starts on its own. If the shared snapshot can't be reached, the page falls back to live data tracked only in your browser.

Reliability score: Once a city has ~3 days of observations, it gets a long-run reliability rating that keeps averaging in as data accumulates. The headline is availability — the share of customer-time with power (an ASAI-style measure) — graded A+ to F, alongside how often it has any outage and its worst severity seen. It's built only from collected snapshots, so early on it reflects whatever storms fell in the window and stabilizes over weeks.

Weather events: The tracker pulls active National Weather Service alerts for the area and tags each outage as weather-related or not. An outage with no current/recent NWS alert and no active regional storm is flagged blue-sky — a possible infrastructure issue rather than storm damage. Storm aftermath (outages lingering after an alert expires) isn't counted as blue-sky.

Privacy: No accounts, cookies, analytics, or tracking. The shared snapshot is fetched from GitHub; map tiles and ZIP lookups from OpenStreetMap/CARTO and zippopotam.us; in fallback mode, live data from FirstEnergy (kubra.io) and Cleveland Public Power's ArcGIS. Those services (like any website) see your IP address. Anything saved locally stays in your browser's localStorage.

Open data: the shared dataset is public JSON, refreshed ~every 15 minutes — tracker-data/state.json. Reuse it freely (it includes current outages, peaks, histories, reliability, ETR-accuracy, and weather).

Report an outage: FirstEnergy — 1-888-LIGHTSS (1-888-544-4877) / firstenergycorp.com; Cleveland Public Power — (216) 664-3156 / cpp.org.

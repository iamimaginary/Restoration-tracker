About this tracker: A self-contained dashboard for monitoring FirstEnergy power-restoration progress across Northeast Ohio counties. All data is entered/imported and stored locally in your browser (localStorage) — nothing is sent anywhere.

Live official data: FirstEnergy's real-time outage feed cannot be embedded directly due to cross-origin (CORS) restrictions. For live numbers, use the official Ohio outage map or report an outage at firstenergycorp.com / call 1-888-LIGHTSS (1-888-544-4877). Update the cards here as new figures are published.

Live data: Pulls FirstEnergy's official Ohio Storm Center feed (KÜBRA, refreshed ~every 15 min) directly in your browser and tracks restoration per county and per city/township. "% restored" is measured against the peak outage this tracker has observed for each location, so leave the page open during a storm to watch the curves come down. Click any county card to drill into its cities/townships, or explore the map. Peaks & trend history are saved in localStorage.

NE Ohio Customers Without Power — Restoration Trend building from live snapshots…

Live data: This dashboard pulls FirstEnergy's official Ohio Storm Center outage feed (KÜBRA, refreshed by FirstEnergy roughly every 15 minutes) directly in your browser and tracks restoration progress per county. "% restored" is measured against the peak outage this tracker has observed for each county, so leave the page open during a storm to watch the curve come down. Peaks and the trend history are saved in your browser's localStorage.

Report an outage: 1-888-LIGHTSS (1-888-544-4877) or firstenergycorp.com. If the live feed can't be reached (network/feed change), the last fetched figures stay on screen.

Not affiliated with or endorsed by FirstEnergy Corp. For official, authoritative outage information always refer to FirstEnergy directly.

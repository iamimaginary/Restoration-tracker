How it works: Reads two public feeds directly in your browser. FirstEnergy (Ohio Storm Center, refreshed ~every 15 min) reports customers out per county and per city/township. Cleveland Public Power reports which distribution feeders are out of service plus an approximate count of affected accounts (no per-customer counts). "% restored" is measured against the peak each source has shown since you opened the page, so a fresh visit mid-storm starts near 0% and the curves fall as you keep it open. Click any FirstEnergy county card to drill into its cities/townships, search for a city or ZIP, or explore the map.

Privacy: No accounts, cookies, analytics, or tracking. Observed peaks and trend history stay on your device in localStorage and are never sent anywhere. Live data, map tiles, and ZIP lookups are fetched from FirstEnergy (kubra.io), Cleveland Public Power's ArcGIS, OpenStreetMap/CARTO, and zippopotam.us, which (like any website) see your IP address.

Report an outage: FirstEnergy — 1-888-LIGHTSS (1-888-544-4877) / firstenergycorp.com; Cleveland Public Power — (216) 664-3156 / cpp.org.
